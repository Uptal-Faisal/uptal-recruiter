How to use this. Every user-facing string in the build, English and Arabic side by side. Click any cell and type — edits turn amber and are saved automatically in this browser, so you can close the tab and come back. Use the notes panel below for anything free-form. When you are done: Save as HTML downloads this whole document with your edits baked in (still editable, works offline); Save as PDF prints what is currently visible — tap “Edited only” first to produce a PDF of just your changes; Export changes downloads a JSON of only your edits for the engineers to apply verbatim. Text like <b>…</b> or {n} is formatting/placeholders — keep those tokens intact.
